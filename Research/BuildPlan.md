# Implementation and acceptance

1. Bundle the original campaign artwork locally. A cold launch opens the Grand Central station wall in airplane mode. Use the final four-screen visual study for the wall, Explore and profile. Earlier frames show an alternative three-icon navigation strip.
2. Build the station wall and campaign sheet. Rotated poster frames have independent targets and count badges. The selected campaign exposes its horizontal artwork variants and a Reframe button.
3. Keep the artwork, normalized drawing coordinates, rich text, stickers and generation jobs in one persistent document. Center snapping, undo, redo and draft recovery must work on device.
4. Implement the asynchronous station sequence. Circling a region opens a keyboard prompt. A pending region stays visible while other tools work. Resolve the job against its original position, including after relaunch. Use an offline clock and artwork processor as the baseline. Optional live MTA timing and Image Playground connect to available services.
5. Publish locally, show the result framed, share a rendered image and return the work to the station wall. Explore supports positive votes, distinct awards and saved art. A profile displays authored work and resumable drafts.
6. Exercise the main sequence in the iPhone simulator and compare screenshots to the wall, tool and detail references.
